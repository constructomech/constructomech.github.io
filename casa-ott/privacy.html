Casa Ott privacy policy

Casa Ott is a personal home app used only by the household that runs it.

What it accesses. With the household's permission, Casa Ott reads the state of their own Nest thermostat (temperature, humidity, mode and settings) through Google's Smart Device Management API, and changes its settings when they ask it to.

Where data goes. That data is used only to show the thermostat in the app and to apply the household's changes. It stays on the household's own home server. It is not sold, shared, or sent to anyone else, and is not used for advertising or analytics.

Removing access. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or in the Nest app.

Last updated November 2026.
